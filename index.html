Interests

I like to start things and build things.

I started hacking with Ruby on Rails back in Version 1.2 and love Ruby and it's microframeworks, especially Sinatra.

I've also produced front-end apps in JavaScript, using Backbone.js and custom frameworks, and iOS apps in Objective-C. I'm warming to Angular.js.

When I get a chance I like to tinker with Max/MSP and Processing. I'm passionate about functional programming and have deployed a little Haskell to production.

I also love music, books, fitness and good food.
